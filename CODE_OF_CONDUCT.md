# Code of Conduct

This project has adopted the [.NET Foundation Code of Conduct](https://dotnetfoundation.org/about/policies/code-of-conduct), which is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).

It applies everywhere the project happens: this repository, its issues and pull requests, the documentation, and the project's [Telegram chat](https://t.me/prethinkdev).

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Criticise code, not the person who wrote it. Remember that most people here are giving up their own time, and that many of them are writing in a language that is not their first.

## Reporting

If you see behaviour that breaks this, or that makes you uncomfortable, report it to the maintainer directly at [@prethink](https://t.me/prethink) on Telegram, or by email to `ilia-samarin@bk.ru`.

Reports are handled privately. You will not be asked to confront the person yourself.

If the report concerns the maintainer, or you would rather it went to someone outside the project, the .NET Foundation accepts reports at `conduct@dotnetfoundation.org`.
